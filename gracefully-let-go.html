Gracefully let go 

｜Gracefully let go・優雅地放下｜

Letting go is never easy. It demands determination, perseverance, and courage. To let go with grace and gentleness, without emotional disturbance, is as difficult as keeping a feather suspended in the same place in mid-air. The eagle feather, a symbol of courage, embodies the quiet strength of letting go—firm yet gentle, resilient yet soft.

「放下」任何人、事、物，本就不易，需要強大的決心、毅力與勇氣才能完成。要做到優雅、輕柔，沒有任何情緒起伏，就像是試圖把羽毛維持漂浮在半空中同個位置般困難。

以象徵勇氣的鷹羽，體現「優雅地放下」剛柔並濟的堅毅與柔軟。 

L 285 x D 110 x H 110 mm
Camphor wood 樟木
