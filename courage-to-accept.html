Courage to accept

｜Courage to accept 坦然接受的勇氣｜

How can one keep moving forward with a smile, even when life leaves countless scars? The answer lies in the courage of acceptance. Like the polar bear, we must embody independence and resilience, trusting that even in harsh conditions, we can still live with ease. Every wound in life is both a lesson and a reminder, urging us to face and to accept. By believing that every crisis can turn into opportunity, and by giving our utmost effort, we free ourselves from regret, even in failure. These experiences become nourishment, shaping a graceful strength that allows us to rise lightly after each fall. For falling itself is never the problem—what matters is rising again with calm acceptance.

After all, in life, everything short of death is only a scratch.

如何在滿身傷痕的生活中，仍能微笑前行？關鍵在於「坦然接受的勇氣」。如同北極熊般獨立堅韌，在艱難環境中也相信能自在生存。生活的每道傷痕，都是學習與提醒，更是面對與接受的契機。唯有不斷說服自己：危機就是轉機，凡事竭盡全力，才能在失敗時無愧於心。這些歷練與積累，終將成為養分，使我們看似輕盈而優雅地活著。跌倒從不是問題，重點在於心境的坦然，能夠拍拍膝上的灰塵，再度站起，繼續前行。

畢竟生命除了死亡，一切都只是擦傷。
