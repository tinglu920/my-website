《Situation：處境》

《Situation：處境》

｜Main Concept｜

Situation: As Condition examines the states people inevitably confront throughout life, and the choices made within them.

Life is shaped by countless different “situations,” none of which offers a single right answer. Faced with the same circumstance, each person may make a different choice. We do not always fail to see; more often, we see, recognise, and then choose to overlook—sometimes by choice, sometimes because we have no other way.

The four works take the floor, wall, materiality, and emotion as carriers, beginning with subtle and persistent changes within everyday life. They examine the tensions between the visible and the invisible, the outer and the inner, awareness and neglect. Traces left unattended and emotions set aside do not disappear. Through neglect and delay, they continue to accumulate and quietly expand until they become impossible to avoid.

Seeing, therefore, is not simply an act of perception. It is a decision about what we see, what we acknowledge, and what we choose to leave unseen.

｜主要概念｜

關注人在人生中不得不面對的狀態，以及身處其中所做的選擇。

人生由無數不同的「處境」構成，而處境從來沒有唯一的正確答案。面對同一件事，每個人都可能做出不同的選擇。人並非總是沒有看見；更多時候，我們看見、察覺，然後選擇忽略——有時出於選擇，有時則因為不得不然。

四件作品以地面、牆面、物質與情緒為載體，從日常生活中那些細微而持續的變化出發，探討「可見」與「不可見」、「外在」與「內在」、「察覺」與「忽略」之間的張力。那些未被處理的痕跡與被擱置的情緒並不因此消失；它們在忽略與延遲之中持續累積，並安靜地擴張，直到成為無法迴避的存在。

因此，觀看不只是感知，而是決定我們看見什麼、承認什麼，以及選擇留下什麼不可見。

｜Turning a Blind Eye・視而不見｜

A subtle distortion appears on the floor, resembling faint irregularities in everyday life. It exists, yet remains barely visible; it is seen, yet easily ignored. Over time, turning a blind eye becomes a strategy—allowing one to live with others or oneself. Sometimes, turning a blind eye can be a choice—or simply a condition one must endure. One has to turn a blind eye to so much of oneself in order to get through life. 

地面出現細微變形，如同日常中不易察覺的異樣。它存在，卻不夠明顯；被看見，卻容易被忽略。視而不見有時是一種策略，使人得以與他人或自我共處；或是一種選擇；有時是一種不得不然的處境。有時，為了度過一生，人不得不對自身那麼多的東西視而不見。

｜Unattended Signs・未被處理的跡象｜

Wallpaper slowly peeling away, its fragments falling like subtle traces of time. Nothing breaks dramatically; It simply happens within the ordinary. People pass by, pause, and rarely notice. The wall remains silent. Many fractures expand in this same quiet way. What is left unattended does not vanish—it accumulates through delay and neglect, eventually becoming something unavoidable.

牆紙緩慢剝落，碎片落在地面，像時間留下的微小痕跡。沒有戲劇化地破裂，只在日常中悄然發生。人們經過、停留，卻很少真正察覺。牆面依舊保持沉默。而許多裂縫正是在這樣的沉默裡擴大。那些未被處理的跡象，並不消失，而是在忽視與延遲之中持續累積，最終成為難以回避的現實。

｜Evidence in Tears・流動的證據｜

Beginning with a single tear, the work expands into multiple states of being. Scored ceramic surfaces record repetition and failure; gold leaf marks flashes of achievement; transparent glass suggests clarity after understanding—whether acceptance or release. Tears belong not only to sorrow but also to joy, success, injustice, and emotional impact. What appears as a visible single moment is formed through countless unseen accumulations.

由一滴眼淚延展為多重形式狀態。陶瓷上的刻痕記錄反覆嘗試與挫敗，金箔象徵成就的光芒，玻璃的清透則指向理解之後的瞬間清晰——無論是接納或放手。眼淚不僅來自悲傷，也源於喜悅、委屈與被觸動的時刻。那些被看見的瞬間，實則由無數未被察覺的情緒層層累積而成。

｜Beneath Appearances・表象之下｜ 

A seemingly formidable polar bear sits on a drifting ice floe. Its hard exterior contrasts with a soft interior that slowly emerges through cracks. It’s a simple but often overlooked idea: what we see is usually only the surface. When perception stops there, understanding can easily become distorted. What truly defines a person tends to remain unseen. Exterior and interior are not opposites; they simply require time to be understood.

看似強大的北極熊靜坐於浮冰之上，外殼堅硬，內部柔軟，並從裂縫中緩慢流出。這是簡單卻常被忽略的事：我們往往只看見表面。當理解停留於表面，就容易產生偏差與誤判。真正構成一個人的部分，往往隱藏在不被注意之處。外與內並非對立，只是需要時間去被看見。
