The Backbone

｜The Backbone・後盾｜

Life can sometimes feel like an unpredictable storm. When one life is held by another, where does that strength truly come from? A child may become the reason a parent keeps moving forward, while a parent may be a child’s first source of support in facing the world. 

Perhaps a backbone is not simply about protecting or being protected, but about becoming the strength that allows another to keep going. Perhaps the true backbone has always been each other.

 

生命有時像一場無法預測的風雨。當一個生命被另一個生命托住，支撐究竟來自哪裡？孩子是父母持續向前的理由，父母也是孩子面對世界時最初的依靠。

所謂後盾，或許並不只是保護與被保護的關係，而是在彼此的存在之中，成為繼續走下去的力量。真正的後盾，也許從來都是彼此。

W19.5 x D16.5 x H48.5 cm
Jindai wood 神代木
