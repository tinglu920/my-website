Unattended Signs

｜Unattended Signs・未被處理的跡象｜

Wallpaper slowly peeling away, its fragments falling like subtle traces of time. Nothing breaks dramatically; It simply happens within the ordinary. People pass by, pause, and rarely notice. The wall remains silent. Many fractures expand in this same quiet way. What is left unattended does not vanish—it accumulates through delay and neglect, eventually becoming something unavoidable.

牆紙緩慢剝落，碎片落在地面，像時間留下的微小痕跡。沒有戲劇化地破裂，只在日常中悄然發生。人們經過、停留，卻很少真正察覺。牆面依舊保持沉默。而許多裂縫正是在這樣的沉默裡擴大。那些未被處理的跡象，並不消失，而是在忽視與延遲之中持續累積，最終成為難以回避的現實。
