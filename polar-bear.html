Fearless

｜Fearless・無所畏懼｜

Life is about being fearless and moving forward—only then can we see a different landscape.

With the head of a lion and the body of a dragon, the mythical creature rides the waves, symbolizing courage and unwavering will. Using self-made slip, white slip coating, and four layered glazes, the work captures the ever-changing form of the sea, distilling the strength and conviction to charge ahead without fear.

人生就是要無所畏懼，勇往直前，才能看見不一樣的風光！

獅首龍身，乘浪而行。奇獸躍於巨浪之巔，象徵無畏與堅定意志。以自製化妝土、粉引與四種釉藥交疊，雕塑變幻莫測的浪花，凝鍊出勇往直前的力量與信念。
