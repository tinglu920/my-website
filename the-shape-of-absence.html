The Shape of Absence

｜The Shape of Absence・無形之形｜

A girl wears a Viking helmet, symbol of courage. In her arms is no real cat, but a silhouette rendered in Nashiji-urushi lacquer—a tribute to a cat who shared nearly twenty years of life. After her passing, memories flash like distant fireworks—brief, bright, and lingering. This shadow is more than a pet’s form; it marks the imprint of all we’ve loved and lost. When someone is gone, does love remain? This work quietly asks: what truly exists, and what is unseen yet endures? In loss, we search for meaning—and gather the courage to keep walking.

小女孩戴著象徵勇氣的維京帽，懷中所抱，並非真實之物，而是一道以梨子地漆描繪的貓影——一隻陪伴將近二十年的老貓。牠離開後，記憶如煙火乍現，殘光閃爍，卻揮之不去。這道剪影，不只是一隻貓的輪廓，更是每段逝去關係的痕跡。當摯愛不再，愛本身是否仍存在？什麼是真正的存在？什麼是無形卻永不消失的？在失去中尋找答案，從悲傷中汲取前行的勇氣。

W31 x D24 x H67 cm
Camphor wood, Gold leaf, Nashiji-urushi 樟木、金箔、梨子地漆
