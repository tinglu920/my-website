Unyielding Will

｜Unyielding Will 頑強的意志力｜

This wheel-thrown Daruma holds a hidden bell that rings in an instant—like a prayer calling forth inner strength. The body shifts from ivory to a gradient of deep indigo and slate blue, reflecting emotional transitions during adversity. Gold leaf highlights the focused gaze, while a hand-painted mountain on the back symbolizes faith and willpower. Smoke firing crackles mark life’s trials, urging us to rise again from every sudden fall. 

這件手拉胚達摩，內藏鈴鐺，搖晃時在一瞬間響起的聲音，像喚神般喚醒內在的力量。作品由米白色起，逐漸轉為深藍至灰藍的青花漸層，象徵面對困境時心境的轉化與沉澱。達摩的雙眼周圍貼有金箔，散發堅定神采；背部手繪的青花山脈象徵堅持信念與不動如山的意志。煙燻燒結的細微裂紋，是生命歷練的痕跡，提醒我們：在任何一瞬的挫敗後，都要再站起來。

L25 x W25 x H30
Ceramic, gold leaf. Smoke firing. 陶瓷、金箔，煙燻燒成
