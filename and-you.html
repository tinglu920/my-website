And You? 

｜And You? 你呢?｜

Using the “whale fall” as metaphor, the work reflects on the cycle of life and renewal. When a whale sinks into the deep, its body becomes nourishment for the ocean, sustaining ecosystems for decades. Death, then, is no longer an end, but a quiet act of exchange and giving. When our own lives come to stillness, can we too leave something that sustains others? Can the weight we carry in existence become a force of continuation beyond ourselves? And you? Ｗhat do you want to leave to the world?

以「鯨落」為隱喻，探討生命的遞循與再生。「鯨落」——當巨鯨死後沈入海底，其軀體化為萬物的養分，能滋養整個生態系統數十年，延續生命於死亡之後。這是一場關於消逝與延續的對話，關於存在的重量與意義。死亡不再是終點，而是一場持續的交換與給予。當一切歸於靜止，我們能否仍為世界留下養分？你呢？你想能為世界留下什麼？

W17 x D9 x H25 cm
Lime wood, walnut, acrylic paint 椴木、胡桃木、壓克力顏料
