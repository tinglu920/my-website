Moose, please help!

｜Moose, please help! 駝鹿幫幫忙！｜

“Moose, please help!” is a ceramic sculpture born from personal limitation and symbolic longing. During a period of physical illness that kept me from walking, I yearned for the mountains—spaces of freedom and memory. I created this moose to hike for me, to carry a backpack and gather the mushrooms I love. With its sturdy legs, it walks the trail I can’t, fulfilling a wish I could no longer act on. This piece is not only about climbing; it’s about resilience, surrogacy, and tenderness in the face of constraint. It’s a quiet defiance—one footstep at a time.

「駝鹿幫幫忙」是一件融合個人體驗與象徵意義的陶瓷雕塑。創作期間，我正被身體的慢性病困住，難以行走或登山。山林，是我記憶中最自由的場域，香菇，是我童年與味覺的連結。於是我打造這隻駝鹿，背著登山包，緩緩走進山林，用牠的四肢代替我的腳，替我完成無法親身實踐的願望。它不只是攀登，而是在幫我重拾一段通往自然與內在的路。這件作品，是一場無聲的接力，也是對身體限制的一種溫柔對抗。
