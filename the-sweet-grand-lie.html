The Sweet Grand Lie

｜The Sweet Grand Lie・甜美大謊言｜

“Wonderful World” stands as a metaphor of truth wrapped in sweet packagings. Its charm disarms, turning desire into justification. When happiness becomes a marketing language, overconsumption slips quietly into normal life. Recycling slogans echo, yet plastics still rise, oceans still choke, and e-waste keeps spreading. Layered without reflection, it grows into a weight the planet can no longer bear. We live within this polished illusion, forgetting who pays for it. The work is not an accusation but a reminder: every purchase defines where awareness begins. knowing what we need, not what we’re told to want.

「Wonderful World美好世界」暗喻被糖衣包裹的真相。甜美的外表讓人放下戒心，也讓慾望合理化。當幸福成為銷售語言，過度消費便被視為日常的一部分。回收的口號遍地，卻無法掩蓋塑膠堆積、海洋受損、電子廢棄物流竄的現實。在不加反思地堆疊下，成為地球難以承受的重量。我們在「美好世界」的幻象裡安然生活，卻忘了誰在為此買單。這件作品不是指責，而是一種提醒——在每一次消費之前，辨別真正的需要與被塑造的慾望。
