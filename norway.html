Hare with straight flush

A hare, dressed in a soft gray-blue hoodie, sits cross-legged, quietly holding a set of a perfect royal flush. Yet the scene isn’t one of celebration, but of calm reflection. This piece speaks to a deeper truth: life isn’t defined by privilege or luck, but by how we use what we’re given. The hare becomes an allegory for clarity, choice, and quiet strength in the face of chance. It is a story of wisdom over circumstance.
