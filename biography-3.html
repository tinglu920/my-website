｜Artist Biography・藝術家簡介｜
 
With over three decades of experience in fashion design, publishing and creative work for luxury brands, she began working with ceramics in 2017. A turning point in 2019 led her to reconsider time, existence and the human condition. In 2022, she returned to university to study ceramics, wood carving and sculpture, while continuing her artistic practice alongside her studies.
 
Her practice centres on sculpture and extends across ceramics, wood and mixed media. Through the material qualities and transformations of different media, she explores how existence becomes perceptible and how time leaves traces upon things. Texture, weight, fragility and transformation become ways of considering how form can hold time, and how existence is perceived between what is visible and what is not.
 
具超過三十年時尚設計、出版與精品品牌創意經驗。2017年開始接觸陶藝，2019年的人生轉折促使其重新思考時間、存在與人的處境。2022年重返校園，系統性研究陶瓷、木雕與雕塑技術，目前持續大學學業與藝術創作。
 
創作以雕塑為核心，延伸至陶瓷、木材與複合媒材。透過不同材料的物質性與變化，探索存在如何被感知，以及時間如何在物件上留下痕跡；材料的質地、重量、脆弱性與變化，也成為思考形體如何承載時間，以及存在如何在可見與不可見之間被感知的方式。

｜Artist's Statement・創作自述｜
 
My practice begins with a fundamental question: how do we know that something exists?
 
Existence and non-existence are not necessarily opposites; they may occur simultaneously through traces. Indentations, wear, cracks, distortions, remnants and absences are not merely material changes, but evidence of time passing. My work is therefore concerned not with how an object can be preserved intact, but with how it changes through time, and how existence becomes perceptible between what is visible and what is not.
 
I work through sculpture across ceramics, wood and mixed media, allowing the qualities and transformations of each material to become part of the inquiry. Through form, trace and space, I seek to give perceptible form to experiences that cannot otherwise be seen, allowing looking to move beyond the surface.
 
我的創作從一個基本問題出發：我們如何知道某件事存在？
 
「存在」與「不存在」並非彼此對立，而可能同時透過痕跡發生。凹陷、磨損、裂痕、變形、殘留與空缺，不只是物質的變化，也是時間經過的證據。作品因此關注的不是物件如何被完整保存，而是它如何在時間中改變，以及存在如何在可見與不可見之間被感知。
 
我以陶瓷、木材及複合媒材進行雕塑創作，讓材料本身的質地、重量、脆弱性與變化成為思考的一部分。透過形體、痕跡與空間，我試圖讓不可見的經驗獲得可被感知的形式，使觀看不再停留於表面。
