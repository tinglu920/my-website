｜Artist Biography・藝術家簡介｜
 

Ting-Ying Lu brings over three decades of experience in fashion design, publishing and creative work for luxury brands. She began working with ceramics in 2017. A turning point in 2019 prompted a reconsideration of time, existence and how life is lived. In 2022, she returned to university to study ceramics, wood carving and sculpture, continuing her studies alongside an evolving artistic practice.

Working across sculpture, ceramics, wood, photography, cyanotype, found objects and mixed media, her practice explores the relationships between material, form and time. Through the physical qualities and transformations of different materials, she examines how existence becomes perceptible and how traces accumulate, change or disappear over time.

 
具超過三十年時尚設計、出版與精品品牌創意經驗。2017年開始接觸陶藝，2019年的人生轉折促使其重新思考時間、存在與生命的意義。2022年重返校園，系統性研習陶瓷、木雕與雕塑技術，持續在學業與個人藝術實踐之間探索與發展。

創作以雕塑為主要方向，涵蓋陶瓷、木材、攝影、藍曬、現成物與複合媒材。透過不同材料的物質性與變化，探索形體、材料與時間的關係，以及存在如何被感知，痕跡又如何隨時間累積、改變或消逝。

｜Artist's Statement・創作自述｜
 

My practice begins with a fundamental question: how do we know that something exists?

Existence and absence are not necessarily opposites. They may coexist through traces: indentations, wear, cracks, distortions, remnants and voids. These are not merely physical changes, but evidence of time passing and of something having been there. What is absent may continue to exist through what it leaves behind.

Working across ceramics, wood and mixed media, I explore how materials register time through their texture, weight, fragility and transformation. The material is not simply a means of giving form to an idea; its physical properties and changes are part of the inquiry itself. My work is less concerned with preserving an object intact than with how it changes through time, and what remains after that change.

Through form, trace and space, I seek to make invisible experiences perceptible, drawing attention to what is overlooked, absent or difficult to see. Looking, in this sense, is not simply about what lies on the surface, but about what the surface reveals, conceals and leaves behind.

我的創作從一個基本問題出發：我們如何知道某件事存在？

存在與缺席並非必然對立，而可能透過痕跡同時發生。凹陷、磨損、裂痕、變形、殘留與空缺，不只是物質的變化，也是時間經過，以及某種事物曾經存在的證據。即使事物已經離開，它仍可能透過留下的痕跡持續存在。

創作跨越陶瓷、木材與複合媒材，透過材料的質地、重量、脆弱性與變化，探索時間如何作用於物件。材料不只是賦予概念形體的媒介，其本身的物質特性與變化，也是思考的一部分。作品關注的不是物件如何被完整保存，而是它如何在時間中改變，以及改變之後留下了什麼。

透過形體、痕跡與空間，試圖讓不可見的經驗獲得可被感知的形式，並將目光引向那些被忽略、缺席或難以看見的事物。觀看因此不再只是停留於表面，而是進一步思考表面所揭露、遮蔽與留下的痕跡。
